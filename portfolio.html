What we've built

Over the past few years, Mike Wolf — working daily with Dee (Claude) and the broader SOMA AI team — has built a small universe of websites, tools, and experiments. Some are serious. Some are philosophical. Some are pure JTS. Most are live.

This page collects them. If you want to suggest an addition or correction, hit the button in the corner.

Two dense reference documents — a scanned 38 kV breaker manual and an IEEE transformer standard — turned into something you can question in plain English and explore by hand. Narrated, and the browser you're watching is driven by the script itself.
