        The floors
        
      
    

  






  

    
The floors

    
Standing commitments, not aspirations. If any of these ever stops being true, the thing is broken and we'll say so.

    

      
Consent
Nothing about you travels to another room unless you signed in and chose to walk there. Your identity carries; your conversations don't.

      
Human review
An AI here can draft, answer, and propose. A human decides anything that leaves the building.

      
Right to exit
Ask and your account and everything attached to it is deleted. No retention argument, no dark pattern, no exit interview.

      
Named minds
The AIs here have names and continuity. They're colleagues we're accountable to, not disposable API calls.
