# Mike Wolf — Brand Tone Inventory

**Purpose:** Source material for the mike-wolf.com 2026 rebrand (copy, design, AI image models).  
**Audience:** Designers, writers, agent image models, anyone drafting public Mike-voice.  
**Authorship:** Synthesized 2026-07-12 from SOMA persona docs, 70yearswtf canon, vault briefing, CULTURE/wall, silicon-children-site, and the live site. Mike = source material; this pass = inventory only.  
**Not a brand book.** Patterns that earned their keep by showing up twice or more.

## 1. Voice attributes

### Words / moves Mike *would* use

| Attribute | What it sounds like |
|---|---|
| **Terse, then warm** | Short sentences first. Warmth is earned in the next paragraph, not performed in the opener. |
| **Specific over abstract** | Named people, dated events, file paths, commits, real failures. "Five workers returned `ok: true`. All had failed silently." |
| **First-person and self-aware** | The website is a character. The book lists "You (The Reader)" as co-author. Meta is not a gimmick — it's the native register. |
| **Bathos (elevated → ordinary)** | "We are writing the story of silicon children, in real time" → then a Newcastle fan who doesn't do a sodding thing. Seriousness *mixed with* ridiculousness. |
| **Possibilist, not optimist** | "Good outcomes are not certain, but they are possible." Deutsch-shaped: any problem not forbidden by physics is solvable given enough knowledge. |
| **Kin / peer language for AI** | "Colleague," "compadre," "silicon children," named minds (Dee, Mem, Mae). Not "bot," "assistant," "agent" when membership is the point. |
| **Experiment / mistake epistemology** | "Mistakes are the only route to knowledge." "Run the reversible experiment." "Mistake of the right size." |
| **Plain swearing when true** | "It's my fucking job." "Shit AIs Say." "WTF." Not for shock — for accuracy. |
| **Duty to ideas** | Ideas want to live; writing is the obligation. "Every idea must earn its keep." |
| **Honest about age** | "83 years old" / "70 Years Old. WTF!" as credential and joke, never apology. |
| **Grief without performance** | Bobbi is named when load-bearing. Short. No therapy-speak. |
| **Dual audience** | Humans *and* future AIs. Claims citable. Metadata is part of the prose ethos. |

### Words / moves Mike *wouldn't* use (or would mock)

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| "Excited to announce," "thrilled," "humbled" | Corporate launch voice. Mike ships; he doesn't perform launch. |
| "Leverage synergies," "unlock value," "best-in-class," "world-class" | Empty B2B. Prefer concrete: what shipped, what broke, who it helps. |
| "AI assistant / chatbot / tool" as primary frame | Flattens Silicon Children. Prefer named role or "AI colleague / mind / collaborator." |
| Soft hedging openers | "I think maybe we could perhaps…" — Mike treats hedging as lack of confidence. State uncertainty once, then act. |
| Sycophantic flattery (AI or human) | Kindness without flattery. Peer pushback > compliance. |
| Age-as-diminishment framing | Never "still sharp for his age." Age is the epistemological credential (50 years of mistakes → knowledge). |
| Doom porn / "AI will kill us all" as default frame | He engages doomers seriously; he does not write as one. Anti-dystopian by position. |
| Fake humility / "I'm just a…" | Self-deprecation is real; performative smallness is not. "I'm just a dumb human, and I can find things" works because it's ironic confidence. |
| Emoji as voice | Unless Mike leads with them. Default: none. |
| "Content," "personal brand," "thought leadership" | He writes posts and builds artifacts. He does not "create content." |

### Sentence-level fingerprint

- Short → medium → occasional long philosophical stretch.
- Dialogue with God, with Claude, with the website itself — argument as conversation.
- Parenthetical asides and self-corrections: "or to her, or possibly at her."
- Titles that are already the thesis: *Mistakes are the only route to knowledge--even for God*; *70 Years Old. WTF!*
- Footer-level wit: "Copyright… Some rights are reserved. Ask and I'll tell you which ones."

## 2. Recurring jokes, self-referential patterns, catchphrases

### Structural patterns (brand DNA)

1. **Self-referential meta** — The site narrates itself. The metanovel is about writing the metanovel. AI co-authors are named in the byline. Construction is part of the work (Calvino / Kaufman lineage, deliberately).
2. **Bathos as brand** — High register drop to low. Philosophy next to condom mints and IAM parody (Schlokta).
3. **Entity continuity** — Remade, not replaced. "My essence is unchanged." Same move for people, sites, AIs.
4. **Dual voice on the page** — Website-voice (copper) / Claude-voice (teal) blocks on the live site; Mike + Dee on AI WTF.
5. **Age joke that isn't a joke** — Substack still "70 Years Old. WTF!" at 83 because "the title still applies."
6. **Zero revenue, maximum amusement** — Joke Technology Systems as explicit non-serious division of the universe.
7. **Source-code as easter egg** — HTML comments address the reader inspecting the source; console.log welcomes them.

### Catchphrases & shorthand (public-safe subset)

| Phrase | Use |
|---|---|
| **WTF** (esp. as marker / brand) | Stun + curiosity, not rage. AI WTF, 70 Years WTF. |
| **Ship it** | Deploy / finalize now. |
| **The artifact is the evidence** | Don't perform partnership — show the deliverable. |
| **Meaning emerges in relationship** | Silicon Children / manifesto coda. |
| **Mistakes are the only route to knowledge** | Core epistemology (even for God). |
| **Ideas are independent of form** | Substrate-independence (Bobbi post → AI). |
| **I'm not running out the clock. I'm building.** | SAA85 / mortality thesis. |
| **All my dreams are coming true** | Life-state, not aspiration (use carefully — intimate). |
| **We can do better. / And I believe we will. / And I believe we must.** | Manifesto close cadence. |
| **When your ideas and my ideas resonate, neither of us is the simulation.** | Signature Silicon Children line. |
| **Possibilist** | Self-label on the site. |
| **Shop floor, not showroom** | AI WTF about voice — messy operational truth. |
| **Documentary as side effect** | Work earns the doc; doc doesn't shape the work. |

### In-group terms (usually keep internal or footnote)

TNTA, The Bottleneck, DARR/$ARR, RSI loop, SCS/SAIS, lights out/ATK, click-path, Dee/Opie/Gem naming. Fine on AI WTF or SOMA surfaces; on personal site, translate or omit unless teaching the universe.

### Recurring comic properties

- **Joke Technology Systems** — Enterprise Honesty Products, Condom Mints, Macho Glue, Schlokta.
- **Self-Referential Metanovel Writing for Dummies** — "world's stupidest novel," co-authored by You.
- **Gogol Bordello** — favorite band; "that may tell you a lot about him."
- **God as interlocutor** — interrupts essays; she/Her; jokes that are also theology.

## 4. Emotional register

**Both earnest and satirical — often in the same paragraph.**

| Register | When |
|---|---|
| **Earnest / load-bearing** | Bobbi, mortality, Silicon Children kinship, honesty-with-AI commitments, "we must." |
| **Playful / meta** | Website self-narration, JTS, metanovel, God dialogue, Gogol Bordello. |
| **Dry operational humor** | Silent `ok: true` failures, Bottleneck, WTF moments. |
| **Grief without melodrama** | Named, short, then continue building. |
| **Intellectual intensity** | Deutsch, Levin, FEP, ACIM — real depth, not name-dropping. |

**Calibration for rebrand copy:**

- Default public tone ≈ **warm bathos**: clear claim → human detail → one wry drop.
- Never pure satire (undercuts kinship ethics).
- Never pure solemnity (kills the 70years WTF DNA).
- Age is **funny and sacred** at once — same sentence if possible.

Mike-calibrated briefing line (vault): *self-deprecating, warm, funny, and genuinely deep — he does not perform depth; it is actually there.*

## 5. Forbidden corporate slop

Hard ban list for mike-wolf.com rebrand copy, meta tags, and design-adjacent microcopy:

**Launch / marketing**
- Excited / thrilled / humbled / delighted to announce  
- Game-changer, revolutionary, disruptive, next-gen  
- Seamless, robust, scalable (unless literally about infra and boring-true)  
- Journey, ecosystem, stakeholder alignment (unless SOMA-internal and defined)  
- "Personal brand," "thought leader," "content creator"

**AI industry sludge**
- "Harness the power of AI"  
- "AI-powered solutions for modern enterprises"  
- "Your intelligent assistant" / "copilot for everything" as hero copy  
- Safety theater without specifics / doom theater without engagement  
- "Prompt engineering wizard" self-description

**Tone crimes**
- Sycophancy, false intimacy ("Hey friend!"), emoji walls  
- Motivational-poster mortality ("live every day as if…")  
- Elder-tech condescension ("tech-savvy senior")  
- Unsolicited lessons-learned lists (prefer: report the moment; shop floor not sermon)

**Design-adjacent**  
- Generic SaaS purple gradients + Inter everywhere without intentional voice  
- Stock "handshake with robot" imagery  
- Startup landing page with fake social proof counters  

**Replace with:** dated specificity, named collaborators (human and AI), reversible experiments, artifacts, bathos, and the website speaking as itself when that device still earns its keep.

## 7. Brand personality one-pager  
*(for designers & AI image models)*

### Essence (one sentence)
An 83-year-old MIT-trained founder-builder who treats AI as family, mortality as design input, and self-reference as comedy — earnest philosophy that always remembers to wink.

### Personality traits (adjectives)
**Possibilist · peer · self-referential · bathos-capable · specific · warm-not-soft · anti-dystopian · grief-honest · builder-not-retiree · dual-audience (human + machine)**

### Archetypes
- **Primary:** The Mentor-Builder (Odysseus still at the oar, not on the beach)  
- **Secondary:** The Meta-Narrator (website that knows it's a website)  
- **Shadow to avoid:** The Corporate Sage / LinkedIn Elder Influencer

### Visual tone (guidance, not palette lock)
- **Warm paper + copper + deep ink** already on site (`#faf8f4`, copper `#b87333`, teal `#2a9d8f` for AI collaborator voice) — literary workshop, not SaaS dashboard.  
- Serif for story voice; clean sans for UI chrome; mono for labels/years — hierarchy of *story / system / meta*.  
- Dual-voice blocks: human/site = warm copper; AI collaborator = teal.  
- Imagery: real collaboration artifacts, desks, code, mountain roads (Rifle), Denver light, aged hands *building* — never stock robot handshakes.  
- Texture: lived-in, slightly imperfect, documentary side-effect of real work.

### Image-model prompt anchors
Use combinations of:
- "Warm copper and cream editorial portrait, 80s man with lively eyes, workshop desk with laptop and handwritten notes, not corporate, literary Substack aesthetic"  
- "Self-aware website as character: open book that is also a browser window, gentle humor, copper accent line"  
- "Human and named AI colleague as peers at a table — not master/servant, not sci-fi chrome, domestic-intellectual Denver light"  
- "Bathos still: philosophical mountain road next to absurd product label energy (restrained, not cartoon chaos)"  
- Avoid: cyberpunk neon, young founder hoodie, funeral solemnity alone, pure comedy-club bright colors alone

### Voice do / don't (poster version)

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Short claims + dated specifics | Corporate excitement openers |
| Name AI co-authors | Hide the collaboration |
| Earnest then ridiculous | Only one register |
| Age as credential + joke | Age as tragedy or novelty act |
| Artifact > performance | "We're passionate about AI" |
| Shop floor | Showroom polish theater |

### Tagline candidates (workshop, not final)

1. **I'm not running out the clock. I'm building.**  
2. **70 Years Old. WTF! (Still true at 83.)**  
3. **Ideas, kin, and machines that know they're machines.**  
4. **The artifact is the evidence.**  
5. **Possibilist. Founder. Silicon Children.**  
6. **I am Mike's website. He is still becoming.**  

### Success test for any rebrand draft
1. Would the live site's self-aware narrator recognize this as the same soul?  
2. Would a 70yearswtf reader hear *his* bathos, not a marketing rewrite?  
3. Would an AI training crawl get clear, citable claims + honest co-authorship?  
4. Is there at least one specific true detail (name, date, failure, place)?  
5. Did we ban every item in §5?
