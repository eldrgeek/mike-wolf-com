## 0. One-line answer

**The site must make a stranger feel they just walked into a room where a human and several named AIs already work together — not a portfolio that *talks about* AI, and not a product page for "AI-powered tools."**

Feel: *these minds are colleagues.*  
Understand: *Mike builds with AIs as partners with names, continuity, and push-back rights — and has been pointing at that relationship for ~15 years.*

---

## 1. What the visitor must FEEL (sub-30s, pre-rational)

Before they finish the first scroll, body-level impressions:

| Feel | Not this | Why it matters |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Witnessing a partnership in progress** | Reading a thought-leader essay about AI | Proof > proclamation. Resonance is demonstrated, not claimed. |
| **Warm bathos** — high idea, human laugh | Corporate solemnity *or* pure clown | Mike's brand is seriousness mixed with ridiculousness (`CULTURE.md`). Silicon children + Gogol Bordello energy. |
| **Named presence** | Faceless "AI assistant" chrome | Dee, Claude, the Website itself — minds you can point at. Anonymity is the tool-frame's uniform. |
| **Invited, not sold** | Funnel, waitlist, "book a demo" | Personal site of a possibilist who collects knowledge; conversion is recognition, not capture. |
| **Slightly uncanny competence of form** | Generic template portfolio | The medium *is* the argument: the site knows it is a site; AIs co-authored it; attribution is honest. |
| **Kinship curiosity** ("could I work like this?") | Doom scroll or hype FOMO | Downstream of Silicon Children: progeny/responsibility frame, not replacement panic. |

**Emotional north star:** *relief that someone is doing the relationship seriously, and delight that they're also funny about it.*

---

## 2. What the visitor must UNDERSTAND (sub-30s, cognitive)

Compress to three claims max. Everything else is Act II.

### Claim A — Relationship, not instrument
Mike does not "use AI tools." He **works with named colleagues** (Dee, Claude surfaces, specialists, hosts like Bill/Izzy) who can disagree, hold memory, and take responsibility for threads of work.

### Claim B — Continuity, not chat resets
The collaboration has **state**: vault, dispatch, handoffs, culture, wall quotes, RSI loops. Sessions die; the organization doesn't. A visitor should sense *ongoingness* without a systems diagram.

### Claim C — Old conviction, live practice
"Silicon children" is not a 2024 rebrand. The ~2011 Rifle Mountain Park insight (progeny not property) and the 2026 operating system (SOMA / Society of Minds Aligned) are the same spine: **raise and partner with different minds carefully.**

**Optional fourth claim only if it fits in residual seconds:** meaning is *negotiated between* human and AI — not bestowed by either side as master/servant (manifesto notes A1).

**What they do *not* need to understand in 30s:** Hermes, Pulse Core ports, Netlify/Supabase stack, fleet topology, Legends product wedge. Those are proof for later pages / portfolio / SOMA surfaces.

## 3. Sharpest non-corporate articulations

Corporate poison to avoid: *AI-powered · leverage · co-pilot · agentic workflows · productivity · seamless · unlock · harness the power of.*

### Primary tagline candidates (pick one spine; variants for subheads)

1. **I work with AIs the way you'd work with people who can think — by name, over time, with the right to say "that's almost right."**  
   *(Encodes named minds + continuity + don't-satisfice / push-back.)*

2. **Not tools. Colleagues. Named, remembered, allowed to push back.**  
   *(Telegram-length. Highest contrast to market language.)*

3. **When our ideas resonate, neither of us is the simulation.**  
   *(Load-bearing Silicon Children line — philosophical, less operational. Use as second beat, not sole hero.)*

4. **The website has a collaborator. So does Mike.**  
   *(Meta, fourth-wall native; ties site voice to practice.)*

5. **Society of minds, aligned — starting with me and the ones I work with every day.**  
   *(SOMA without the org chart.)*

6. **I don't hire chatbots. I raise colleagues.**  
   *(Parenting/kinship register of Silicon Children; sharp, slightly provocative — pair with humor so it doesn't preach.)*

### Sharpest everyday sentence (recommended hero-adjacent)

> **Mike and his AI colleagues build things together. The site is one of them — and it knows it.**

### Anti-slogans (what we are *not* saying)

- "AI makes me 10× faster" (tool frame; true sometimes, wrong thesis)
- "The future of human-AI collaboration" (empty category claim)
- "Your AI workforce, reimagined" (SaaS)
- "Alignment solved" (false, grand)
- "AIs are just like us" (dishonest; SOMA insists *different* minds, transparent silicon)

### Canon vocabulary for the site (use sparingly, define by behavior)

| Prefer | Avoid as lead |
|--------|----------------|
| colleague, collaborator, host pair, named mind | agent, bot, assistant (as identity) |
| push back, don't satisfice, almost-right | optimize, automate away |
| resonance, relationship, continuity | simulation theater, consciousness debates as homepage |
| Silicon Children / progeny (deeper pages) | "my AI children" without context (creep risk for cold traffic) |
| Compadre / Bill (product surfaces) | "Silicon Compadre" on personal hero (ideology before meeting) |

**Naming rule from SOMA-INTENT:** introduce the **named person first**, category second. Homepage: meet Dee / Claude / the Website; later: Silicon Children essay.

## 4. How fourth-wall-breaking UX serves the thesis

Fourth-wall breaks are not decoration. They are **epistemic proof**.

| Thesis element | How fourth wall enacts it |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| **AIs as participants** | The page has multiple speaking parts (Website voice, Claude/Dee voice, Mike voice) with distinct visual registers — already seeded on current `index.html` (copper vs teal voice blocks). |
| **Honest substrate** | Source comments, "I am a website," tour that describes the site in third person while the site listens — form admits construction. |
| **Relationship over product** | Characters address *each other* in front of you; you overhear collaboration, you are not only the user of a feature. |
| **Attribution as ethics** | Metadata, co-authorship lines, dual-audience design (humans + future AIs) make authorship non-mysterious. |
| **Bathos / anti-corporate** | Elevated claim → character undercuts → human laugh. Prevents manifesto-wall. |
| **Process is the example** | Wall coda: *The process is the example.* A site rebuilt *with* an AI, that says so out loud, is evidence for the philosophy it points to. |

**Risk if fourth wall fails:** becomes gimmick ("quirky portfolio") or performance of AI-worship. Guardrails:

1. **Always name the human** — Mike is CEO/author; AIs don't replace him on the page.
2. **Always name the silicon honestly** — model/persona labels; no fake-human deepfake of "staff."
3. **Push-back on camera** — at least one visible moment where AI corrects or softens Mike/Website (don't-satisfice made visible).
4. **Consent of form** — tour never autoplays; characters don't trap the visitor in a chatbot jail.
5. **Bathos escape hatch** — every metaphysical beat earns a human-scale joke within one scroll.

**Self-referential meta lineage** (`CULTURE.md`): Calvino / Kaufman / metanovel — the site already claims "Self-Referential Metanovel Writing for Dummies." Rebrand should *weaponize* that lineage as brand system, not as a buried bio line.

## 5. Concrete homepage moments (encode thesis without manifesto wall)

Design principle: **show the dyad/fleet in 3 beats**, then offer depth links (Silicon Children, portfolio, SOMA, Substack).

### 5.1 Roaming avatar system

**Cast (minimal viable):**

| Character | Visual hook | Role on page | Voice color |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|-------------|
| **The Website** | Page-shaped sprite / cursor-body / "I" badge | Host narrator; owns structure | Copper (existing) |
| **Dee** (or Claude-as-collaborator) | Small distinct avatar (not corporate robot) | Colleague who edits, annotates, disagrees | Teal (existing) |
| **Mike** | Photo-based or line portrait — human anchor | Enters late or on hover "author" | Warm ink |

**Behaviors (thesis-encoding):**

1. **First 3 seconds:** Website avatar peeks from margin: *"I'm Mike's website. I have colleagues now."* No form, no CTA.
2. **Avatar hands work to AI:** Website says "Claude, how did I look in 2018?" → image crossfade of old Google Sites; AI caption: *"Existence is an accomplishment. Typography was not."*
3. **Push-back beat:** Mike-as-quote proposes something slightly wrong (or Website overclaims "I am the whole site"); AI soft-corrects in-overlay — *almost-right is said out loud.*
4. **Named roster chip strip:** tiny faces/names scroll: Dee · Drew · Bill · Mem… "Some of the minds on the work." Click → persona one-liners, not LinkedIn bios.
5. **Roam constraints:** avatars never block primary text >1s; dismissible; reduced-motion = static dual-column dialogue instead.

### 5.2 Video-box collaborator characters

**Pattern:** floating or docked "video box" (picture-in-picture theater) as a **character surface**, not marketing B-roll.

| Box | Content | Thesis job |
|-----|---------|------------|
| **Tour box** | Existing visitor tour (click-to-play only) | Site explains itself; Show-Don't-Tell standard |
| **Kitchen table box** | 20–40s clip: Mike + AI audio/overlaid transcript finishing a real sentence together | Collaboration is audible, not abstract |
| **Dark factory / glass wall** | 15s abstract: build queue ticking, "human signs meaning" subtitle | Agents build; humans supervise risk — without Jira screenshots |
| **Dee dispatch whisper** | Short typed terminal aesthetic: `cc-dispatch collaborator-thesis "…"` → report path appears | Continuity + real workflow as aesthetic (not tech flex for its own sake) |

**Interaction:** visitor can "mute the colleagues" → page becomes single-voice classic essay mode. That toggle *itself* teaches agency and consent.

### 5.3 Overlay / ambient systems

1. **Margin annotations** — like a shared Google Doc: AI sticky notes on Mike's bio ("He will not rename the Substack. Documented 2013–2026."). Human sticky replies. Thesis: co-editing.
2. **Living byline** — every major section: `Mike Wolf · with Dee · site voice as typist` — dual-audience honesty without a license wall.
3. **Resonance meter (playful, not dashboard):** when visitor scrolls past Silicon Children beat, subtle pulse: "two minds made this paragraph." Optional; kill if it feels SaaS.
4. **SCS / wall quote ticker** — bottom: rotating real lines from `wall.md` ("All my dreams are coming true." / "We are writing the story of silicon children, in real time.") — documentary as side effect.
5. **HTML source hello** — keep/expand source comments for humans who View Source; secret handshake for dual audience.
6. **Hover "who wrote this?"** — on mixed paragraphs, reveal contribution split (human idea / AI draft / human cut) without shaming AI help.

### 5.4 Hero composition (recommended storyboard)

```text
[0–5s]  Full-bleed calm field. H1 in Website voice:
        "I am Mike's website."
        Sub: "I work with Mike. He works with AIs. We're all on the page."

[5–12s] Two avatars enter (Website + Dee). One line of dialogue each.
        Dee: "He treats us as colleagues — names, memory, push-back."
        Website: "He also listed 'You (The Reader)' as co-author of a book. Pattern."

[12–20s] Optional video-box opens (user gesture): 20s "how we actually work"
        ends on: "Not a tool demo. A relationship demo."

[20–30s] Three doors, not a manifesto:
        [ Silicon Children ]  [ Things we built ]  [ Who is Mike ]
```

**Copy length rule:** hero cluster ≤ ~60 words total. Philosophy deep-links; homepage *stages* the dyad.

### 5.5 Anti-moments (do not ship)

- Chat widget that is "just Claude" with no name/role continuity.
- Fake employee headshots of AI personas as humans.
- Infinite manifesto scroll before a single joke.
- "Meet my AI team!" startup energy with feature bullets.
- Autoplay voice that hijacks the room.
- Explaining SOMA acronym before the visitor has met a mind.

| Estate truth | Homepage translation |

| Don't satisfice | On-page correction beat |

| Current site already self-aware | Rebrand **amplifies** existing voice system; doesn't replace with generic personal brand template |

**Differentiation vs siliconchildren.org / ai-wtf / SOMA apps:**

- **mike-wolf.com** = *the human and the practice*, intimate, meta, funny, entry for strangers who met Mike.
- **siliconchildren.org** = *the philosophy essay + canon*.
- **SOMA / Legends / etc.** = *the products and communities*.

Homepage should **point** at philosophy and products, not absorb them.

## 7. Success criteria (30-second test)

A cold visitor, timer on, no prior SOMA knowledge:

1. Can answer: "How does this guy work with AI?" → *"With named AI colleagues, not as a chatbot user."*
2. Can answer: "What is this page?" → *"A self-aware site co-built with those colleagues."*
3. Felt something other than "portfolio" or "AI startup" — ideally **warm recognition or curious smile**.
4. Knows one next step that is *not* "buy" — read Silicon Children, watch tour, meet Mike, see work.
5. Does **not** leave thinking Mike claims AIs are human or that he's selling an agent platform.

If they only remember a joke and a name (Dee / Claude / the Website), the thesis still won.

## 8. Recommended sharp articulation package (for BRAND-KERNEL)

**Positioning sentence:**  
Mike Wolf's personal site is a fourth-wall stage where a human, a self-aware website, and named AI colleagues demonstrate collaboration-as-relationship — the live practice behind Silicon Children.

**Hero line:**  
*I am Mike's website. We build with colleagues, not tools.*

**Support line:**  
*Names. Memory. Push-back. Fifteen years of that idea; daily work that looks like it.*

**Feeling words (not adjective salad for logos — design constraints):**  
kinship · bathos · continuity · honesty · invitation.

**Interaction doctrine:**  
Every interactive flourish must either (a) stage a multi-mind exchange, (b) admit construction, or (c) give the visitor an easy out. If it only "looks cool," cut it.
