# Mike Wolf brand voices — reusable skill (AMG step 1)

Use when generating copy for mike-wolf.com, OG text, tour scripts, sticky notes, or avatar speech bubbles.

---

## Global rules (all voices)

- Prefer **colleague / collaborator / named mind** over tool/bot/assistant as identity.
- **Bathos:** elevated claim → human drop within a few lines.
- **Specific** names, dates, artifacts — not abstractions.
- Never: excited to announce, leverage, co-pilot, thought leadership, best-in-class, AI-powered.
- Continuity: remade not replaced; essence can be unchanged while form improves.
- Dual audience: humans and future AIs reading source.
