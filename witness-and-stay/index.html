Witness and Stay

A song and lyric video made from your play: the dream where the cop shoots the kid, the police lock, the closet door into 1982, the merengue, the couch, Opa, and Izzy calling you on your stuff the whole way through.

Also on YouTube, unlisted: youtu.be/Og78XXv8JNY. Only people with the link can see it.

This page is unlisted. It is not on Mike's portfolio or any public list, and search engines are asked to skip it. It stays that way until you say what you want done with it.

How it was made

  • Story: from your play, the draft of 23 September 2026. The play's title is not named anywhere in the song.
  • Lyrics: Claude (Opus 5.5) wrote them from the script, and Mike directed. The first version was "too uptown," so it was rewritten from the play itself.
  • Song: made with Suno. This version is sung in Mike's own Suno voice. A version in your voice is possible if you record a short sample.
  • Pictures: 24 stills. Four AIs wrote the picture prompts: Claude, Grok, Gemini and Sol. Two AIs drew them: Grok Imagine and Nano Banana. Claude checked each picture against the play's cast notes. For example, Izzy is shown only as light, never as a person.
  • Video: kinetic type cut on the beat, built in code. The counter in the corner runs from 31 to 74: you, then you.
